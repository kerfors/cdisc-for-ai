# NCIt comparison — archived April 2026 notebooks

Three notebooks that compared COSMoS Biomedical Concept content with NCIt: definitions and synonyms (`COSMoS_BC_NCIt_Compare`), the NCIt source endpoints used by Compare (`COSMoS_BC_NCIt_Source_Probe`, with its response cache), and BC parent chains (`COSMoS_BC_Parent_Resolution`). Last run in April 2026 against the COSMoS 2026-03 package. Archived 2026-10. They are not maintained.

They cannot run as they stand: all three read `cosmos-bc-dss/interim/COSMoS_BC_DSS.xlsx`, the single-sheet flatten retired in May 2026. BC parent chains are now in the graph (`BC_Parents` sheet of `cosmos-graph/interim/COSMoS_Graph.xlsx`), with the instrument chains analysed by `cosmos-graph` notebook 51. The definition and synonym comparison has no successor in the current tracks.

Notebooks, reports and cache are kept as they were. Paths inside them point to their original locations.
